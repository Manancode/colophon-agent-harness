## Video direction

Modernist risograph register (cobalt-grid preset). Cream paper ground, electric
cobalt ink, permanent graph-paper grid at low opacity. Every beat lands on the
grid; nothing floats. Motion is velocity-matched along a single leftward current,
with one reserved Z-push for the product reveal. Type is large and confident;
the captured UI does the proving, never a stock illustration.
